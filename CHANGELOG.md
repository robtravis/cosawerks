# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Arrows:** a Flat Line head with square ends for measurement callouts; a **From Center** animation (both ends grow out from the middle);
  your own heads (draw a path, press + next to Head); Callout, Callout Build and Center Out presets. Open or closed is now read from each head's
  own outline, and the shaft stops at the base of any head, drawn ones included. The Arrow effect has a new internal version, so arrows made
  before this change keep working but need re-applying to get the new choices.

## 0.9.0 (first public build)

Panels: Anchor Point, Align (align, distribute, align to), Trim Paths (staggered builder with ease), Arrows, Type, Stagger,
Ease (with copy and paste), Comps (textless duplicate, batch rename), Buttons (a strip of your own one-click actions).

- **Arrows:** one shape layer, live editable through an "Arrow" effect with real dropdowns; seven heads, tail, draw / draw and off /
  travel, grow-in, presets (built in and your own), follow path, and nulls that drive or follow a path's points.
- **Type:** staggered text animation by character, word or line with looks, ease, delay, duration, in and out; saved looks;
  per-line mask wipes with feather; Slab (fit every line to one width).
- **Stagger:** layers, trim points, keyframes or sequences, with ease spacing.
- **Presets:** export and import arrow presets, Type looks and eases as a `.cwpresets` file.
- Panels follow After Effects' own look, with drag-to-scrub number fields.
