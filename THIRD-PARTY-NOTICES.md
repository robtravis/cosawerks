## Lucide icons (ISC License)

`cep/js/lucide-icons.js` contains icon shapes and search tags generated from the
[Lucide](https://lucide.dev) icon set (npm package `lucide-static`). Lucide is licensed under the ISC License;
some of its icons are derived from Feather (MIT). Both license texts are in `cep/licenses/LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt` and
must stay with any copy of the icons.

Regenerate with `tools/gen-lucide.js` (see the header of that file).

## Build tools (not included in Cosawerks)

`tools/fetch-zxpsigncmd.sh` downloads Adobe's `ZXPSignCmd` from Adobe's public
[CEP-Resources](https://github.com/Adobe-CEP/CEP-Resources) repository, to sign the `.zxp` package. It is a build-time tool, is
git-ignored and is not part of the extension.
